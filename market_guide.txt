MARKET ANALYSIS GUIDE
=====================

STOCK ANALYSIS
--------------

When analyzing a stock, consider:

1. Current stock price
2. Company and business model
3. Recent price movement
4. Market trend
5. Important financial metrics
6. Industry conditions
7. Growth opportunities
8. Key risks

Example stocks:

AAPL - Apple
MSFT - Microsoft
NVDA - NVIDIA
GOOGL - Alphabet
AMZN - Amazon
META - Meta
TSLA - Tesla


CRYPTOCURRENCY ANALYSIS
=======================

When analyzing cryptocurrency, consider:

1. Current price
2. Market capitalization
3. Trading volume
4. Price momentum
5. Market sentiment
6. Technology and use case
7. Adoption
8. Regulatory risks
9. Market volatility

Example cryptocurrencies:

BTC - Bitcoin
ETH - Ethereum
SOL - Solana
ADA - Cardano
XRP - Ripple
DOGE - Dogecoin


IMPORTANT
=========

Current prices should always be retrieved using the
appropriate MCP tool.

Do not invent current market prices.
Clearly distinguish live market data from analysis.
